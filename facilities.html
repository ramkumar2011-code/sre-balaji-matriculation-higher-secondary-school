Our Facilities

We believe a great school environment shapes great students. Our campus is equipped to support learning, health, and recreation every day.

🍽️
School Canteen

Our hygienic and well-equipped canteen provides nutritious, freshly prepared meals and snacks for students every school day. Healthy eating is our priority.

  • ✔ Fresh food daily
  • ✔ Hygienic preparation
  • ✔ Affordable pricing
  • ✔ Vegetarian options
💧
Clean Drinking Water

Clean and safe drinking water is available throughout the campus. We ensure students and staff have access to purified water at all times.

  • ✔ Purified water supply
  • ✔ Multiple water stations
  • ✔ Regularly maintained
  • ✔ Available all day
🏟️
Big Play Ground

Our expansive playground gives students ample space for physical activities, sports, and recreation — promoting health, teamwork, and a joyful school life.

  • ✔ Large open ground
  • ✔ Cricket & football area
  • ✔ Safe play zones
  • ✔ Morning & evening use
🚐
Van Service to All Areas

We offer reliable van transport to and from the school across all nearby neighborhoods, ensuring safe and convenient travel for students each day.

  • ✔ Daily pick-up & drop-off
  • ✔ Covered routes in all areas
  • ✔ Trained drivers & escorts
  • ✔ Flexible timing for all classes
